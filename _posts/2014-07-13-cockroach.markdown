description: Cockroaches exhibit orthokinesis and klinokinesis (movement speed and rates of turning related to the strength of a stimulus) when exposed to light. I created a light-sensitive and touch-sensitive robot with orthokinesis and klinokinesis behaviors. An attached drawing implement recorded the robot's path over time, when allowed to explore an enclosure with non-uniform distribution of ambient light. The piece was selected for The University of Alabama Huntsville's Fall, 2019 Student Art Showcase.
