description: "We created a robotic system in which a mobile manipulation robot performs contact-free vital sign measurement in a healthcare setting. Our work focused on creating a research platform for assured robotic systems, creating redundant, fail-safe systems-of-systems to avoid particularly harmful or undesirable outcomes. I was the Task Lead for the development of our robot's behavior control subsystem (implementing behavior trees), and localization subsystem (implementing Kalman filters and indoor, GPS-like positioning with Ultra Wide Band beacons)."
