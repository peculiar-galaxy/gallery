description: "Unlike traditional digital cameras that create images which sample three wavelengths of light (red, green, and blue), hyperspectral sensors produce images which sample hundreds or thousands of wavelengths across the electromagnetic spectrum, including those outside of the visible light range. These hyperspectral images can be used to 'fingerprint' or identify physical materials (such as limestone, acrylic, aluminum, vegetation, etc.). My Spectral Librarian tool analyzed collections of data produced by arial, drone-mounted sensors, with the goal of classifying materials based on their hyperspectral signatures. The example hyperspectal image below is for representation only. Real data from the Spectral Librarian are classified."
